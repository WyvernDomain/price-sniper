2

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you download.

Is Price Sniper free to use?+

Yes. Price Sniper is free and open source. You can download it from gitscore.cc, inspect the source, and use every feature — including instant buy triggers — at no cost.

Which Windows versions are supported?+

Price Sniper runs natively on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). It's a lightweight desktop app with no external runtime dependencies required beyond the standard installer.

How fast does the refresh loop scan?+

You control the polling interval per watchlist. Shorter intervals catch deals faster; longer intervals are gentler on the market endpoints. Multi-market scanning runs in parallel so adding markets doesn't multiply your wait time.

Are my targets and watchlists sent anywhere?+

No. All watchlists, target prices, rules and price history are stored locally on your machine. Price Sniper has no account system and does not upload your data.
